Privacy Policy – Video Affiliate Uploader

The app uses TikTok Login Kit to obtain an access token for the owner's TikTok account. The token is stored only on the owner's computer and is used solely to upload videos to that account's drafts.

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data, does not read other users' data, and does not use analytics or tracking.

To revoke access, remove the app in TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.
